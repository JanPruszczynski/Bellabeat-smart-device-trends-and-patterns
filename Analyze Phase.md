## 3. Preliminary findings

### Finding 1: Activity levels vary by day of the week

Average daily steps varied across the seven days of the week.

- Saturday recorded the highest average at approximately 7,752 steps.
    
- Sunday recorded the lowest average at approximately 6,607 steps.
    
- Tuesday also showed relatively high activity, averaging approximately 7,570 steps.
    
- Thursday and Friday recorded approximately 7,101 and 7,097 steps, respectively.
    

These results suggest that recorded step counts were generally higher toward the weekend, particularly on Saturday, while Sunday had the lowest average.

### Finding 2: Weekday and weekend step counts are similar overall

When the data was grouped into weekdays and weekends, the results were:

- Weekday average: approximately 7,287 steps per recorded user-day.
    
- Weekend average: approximately 7,188 steps per recorded user-day.
    

The weekday average was approximately 99 steps, or 1.4%, higher than the weekend average. This is a relatively small difference, so the results do not establish a substantial overall difference in step counts between weekdays and weekends.

### Finding 3: Very active minutes were higher on weekends

Users recorded an average of approximately 18.33 very active minutes on weekdays, compared with 19.55 minutes on weekends.

This represents approximately 1.22 additional very active minutes per recorded day on weekends. It suggests that participants may have engaged in slightly more very intense activity during weekends, despite recording marginally fewer steps overall.

### Finding 4: Sedentary minutes were lower on weekends

Average sedentary time was approximately 1,011.82 minutes on weekdays and 984.10 minutes on weekends.

This difference of approximately 27.72 minutes per recorded day suggests that users spent less time classified as sedentary on weekends. However, sedentary minutes should not automatically be interpreted as continuous sitting time or as a direct measure of sleep.

### Finding 5: Observation counts differ across weekdays and weekends

The weekday-versus-weekend comparison included 950 weekday records and 392 weekend records. The seven-day analysis showed between 179 and 199 records per individual weekday.

Although all 35 users appeared in both groups, the number of recorded days differed. Therefore, the averages reflect the available observations, and differences in tracking completeness may influence the results.
